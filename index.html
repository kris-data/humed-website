Who It Helps

Everyday cases (primary implants in common bone)
  • Immediate stability without premium pricing
  • Fast, predictable recovery; universal prosthetic compatibility
  • 11° conical seal → microleakage control & soft-tissue health
  • Post-extraction immediate ready: early stability from the bone-ingrowth chamber
Complex cases (soft/fragile bone & compromised sites)
  • Deep trabecular engagement + variable pitch for gentle, strong grip
  • Bone-ingrowth chamber supports staged regeneration & long-term load sharing
  • Open drilling sequence — no brand-exclusive kits required
  • Helps reduce graft dependence via bone compaction (0.6–0.75 mm pitch near apex)
