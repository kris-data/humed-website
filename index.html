Who It Helps

Everyday cases (primary implants)
  • Immediate stability without premium pricing
  • Predictable recovery; universal prosthetic compatibility
  • 11° conical seal → microleakage control & soft-tissue health
  • Immediate & long-term stability from the ingrowth chamber—supports immediate placement and sustained performance
Complex cases (soft/fragile bone & compromised sites)
  • Deep trabecular engagement + variable pitch for gentle, strong grip
  • Ingrowth chamber supports staged regeneration & 3-D load sharing
  • Open drilling sequence — no brand-exclusive kits required
  • May reduce graft dependence via bone compaction near apex
